Who we are

Indians making Germany home

We’re Filly Belly, a duo of Indians living in Braunschweig, Germany. What started as sharing food finds on Instagram has grown into a travel and lifestyle channel for Indians in Germany and people planning their first Schengen adventure.

We know the visa stress, budget hacks, train apps, and where to find good masala chai in Prague. We speak Hindi and English while learning German.
